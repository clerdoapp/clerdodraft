# Changelog

## 0.5.0
First release.

- Writing: styles, themes, lists, tables with formulas, columns, sections, headers and footers, footnotes, right-to-left paragraphs, autocorrect and smart typing
- More than 400 fonts, every font installed on the computer, and symbol fonts that show the right characters
- About 150 shapes, 52 diagram layouts, 20 chart kinds, 2,300 icons, equations, text boxes and text art with glow, reflection and gradients
- Pictures with crop and adjustments, pictures from a web address, pen drawing and drawing on pictures
- Embed any file; spreadsheets, documents and presentations stay editable in other apps
- 41 templates, including an Arabic letter
- Review: tracked changes including formatting, comments with replies and reactions, document compare, version history, restricted editing by paragraph, digital signatures
- Spelling in English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch and Russian; grammar; word book; hyphenation in 21 languages
- References: contents, captions, citations in five styles, index, table of authorities, cross-references
- Mail merge from spreadsheets, contacts and data files, with envelopes and label sheets
- Work together live, device to device, and share documents as links
- Opens `.docx`, `.doc`, `.odt`, `.rtf`, `.pdf`, `.md`, `.html` and `.txt`, including password-protected files; EMF and WMF pictures are drawn on the page
- Interface in English and Arabic
- Desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux, and a web app that works offline on iPad
